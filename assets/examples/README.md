# Ember lake example

`ember-lake.png` is an original ImageGen illustration created for the PeerBrush website. `ember-lake.psd` was assembled and saved in the real PeerBrush document engine, with color balance, a duplicated reflection layer, a painted raster mask and mask blur. The curated screenshots in `docs/images/website/` capture the native application; the interface is not generated.

Generation mode: new image, opaque background, no reference images. The artwork was imported at document coordinates `[0, 0, 1536, 1024]`.

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original canvas artwork for real PeerBrush image-editor screenshots and a bundled example. Create a beautifully crafted wide landscape digital painting, 3:2 aspect ratio. Scene: a quiet luminous mountain lake at sunset with sculptural dark plum and indigo peaks, a large warm ember-orange sun just above the distant ridge, soft mist bands between mountains, delicate curved foreground reeds, and rippled turquoise-blue water catching thin ivory and copper reflections. Richly layered atmospheric depth, restrained confident painterly brush texture, striking elegant composition and luminous lighting, premium contemporary concept art. The painting must read clearly when shown inside a software window at small sizes. Palette: near-black plum, deep indigo, ember orange, warm ivory, and subtle ice-blue highlights. Produce the art itself edge to edge, no app interface, no picture frame, no borders, no text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. This will be imported into a real editable application; do not draw any UI.
